# The research corpus

> **This corpus is migrating out of this repository.** These are pre-reboot wayfinder deliverables — design studies, evaluations, and register work — and their primary home for deep structure is now the project's research vault, as it already is for the specification corpus under [`docs/gandr/spec/`](../gandr/spec/README.md).
> This repository keeps its code, its tests, and its outward-facing (user-level) documentation.
> **Do not add new design documents here, and prefer editing over growing what is here.** New design content is authored externally; what this repository still needs from a design is a thin decision record stating the outcome and what it binds, not a design document. (Owner direction, 2026-08-07; the migration is deliberate and in progress.)

Documents leave this directory one at a time, and each departure rewires its own inbound references in the same change, so nothing in the tree ever points at a file that is gone.
**A document that has left is listed below rather than silently absent**: a reader who arrives through an old link, an old commit message, or an old citation needs to know that the content was carried somewhere rather than dropped, and under what title to look for it.

Removal follows absorption and never precedes it, and a stale claim in a departing document is corrected in the absorbing copy rather than here — so the absence of a repair commit against a removed file is expected, not an omission.
