## 1. The two inventories, named exactly

On the CCHM side (all §/page refs to the v1 paper):

* **The interval** `I` — the free De Morgan algebra on a discrete infinite set of names: a bounded distributive lattice with `0`, `1`, `∧`, `∨` and an involution `1 − r` satisfying the De Morgan laws; equality in `I` is decidable (§3).
* **Path abstraction** `⟨i⟩ t` — the "lambda-like" binder; a path is a _map out of_ `I`, and `refl` is the degenerate abstraction `1ₐ = ⟨i⟩ a` (§3.1, Fig. 2).
* **The face lattice** `F` — the distributive lattice generated by symbols `(i = 0)`, `(i = 1)` with `(i = 0) ∧ (i = 1) = 0_F`; disjunctive normal forms make its equality decidable; face formulas `φ` are the constraint language of restriction contexts `Γ, φ` and of systems `[φ ↦ u]` (§4.1).
* **Composition** `comp^i A [φ ↦ u] a₀` — the Kan operation, a primitive defined _by induction on the type_ `A`; the paper introduces it precisely because "we need to add composition operations … in order to justify the elimination principle for paths" (the closing line of §3.2, following the singleton-contractibility example; the operation itself is §4).
* **Inversion and connections** — path reversal is the substitution `(i/1−i)`, i.e. the involution itself; the connections `(i/i∧j)` and `(i/i∨j)` are the degenerate squares, and singleton contractibility is the connection square `⟨i⟩ (p i, ⟨j⟩ p (i ∧ j))` (§3.1–§3.2).

On the ua-base side (`iu:docs/spec/DESIGN-ua-base-vocabulary.md`; "design §N" below refers to that document):

* **Codes** with decidable equality, h-sets by Hedberg — while the protypes over them are deliberately not sets (the U3.0c lesson; design §1).
* **The edit polygraph** `ℰ` — dimension 0 the codes, dimension 1 directed edit generators each carrying a designated inverse schema and per-schema round-trip evidence, dimension 2 the oriented rule layer generating the rule congruence `≈ᶜ` (design §4).
* **Grades** — β at replay grade `≈ʳ`, η at rule grade `≈ᶜ`, never `≡` (design §1).
* **Saturation** — the instance stocks of universal properties are modules over the path relation (`actˡ`/`actʳ` with the action laws), stated alphabet-first (design §3); §5 below works this in full.

## 2. The shared strategy: a decidable algebra presents identity

Both systems put a finitely presented algebra with a decidable word problem at the bottom of the identity story, and both owe their tractability to that choice.
CCHM: the free De Morgan algebra `I` (decidable equality, §3) and the face lattice `F` (DNF-decidable, §4.1) keep judgmental checking of the cubical apparatus effective. ua-base: decidable code equality plus a convergent rewriting system on edit strings give path normal forms, hence decidable `≈ᶜ` and a computable `edit` (design §1, §5).
This is the "deeper relationship" the comparison question senses: the two systems are the _same strategy_ executed at two different addresses.

## 3. The fundamental difference is placement

CCHM installs the algebra **in the shape**: `I` is an indexing object, a path is a _function_ out of it, and the identity structure is uniform over an open universe because a kernel primitive (`comp`, defined by induction on the type) forces every type to be Kan. ua-base installs the algebra **in the codes**: `ℰ` is a presentation _of the universe itself_, a path is _data_ — a free directed string of generators modulo the completed rule layer — and elimination is defined by induction on that data.

Identity-as-function versus identity-as-data, with a consequence each:

* In CCHM, constancy of a path is a **property** of a function (`i`-independence), judgmentally invisible: nothing in a term of `Path A a a` says "constant".
* In ua-base, `refl` is a **constructor** — the empty word `[]` (the demonstrator's `Edit.agda` reuses the `Internal.Rewriting` word structure; design §7 frames the same discipline through the `Step*` kit's `here`, a recorded spec-to-code naming drift) — and is therefore pattern-matchable.

Transport makes the same contrast operational: CCHM's transport is `comp` computed by induction on the **type**; ua-base's transport is realization computed by induction on the **generator** — O1 makes every generator iso-valued by construction, each shipping its translator pair and round-trip evidence (design §1, §4).

## 4. The J ledger

**Why CCHM loses J-on-refl.** J is derived from `comp` through singleton contractibility (the connection square, §3.2/§4).
Its computation rule on `refl` reduces to a composition along a degenerate system, and making that vanish is _regularity_: the judgmental equality `transp^i A a₀ = a₀` for `A` independent of `i`. §9.1 states plainly that this equality is absent — "an equality that we cannot expect geometrically in general, as shown in Appendix C" — so the Martin-Löf computation rule holds only propositionally for `Path`.
The obstruction is a feature of the model, not an engineering gap.

**Why ua-base keeps it.** J-β is a definitional unfolding because elimination is structural recursion on the string: `iu:src/Internal/Profunctor/Tabulator/Path.agda` restricts along `refl-sec` (`J-to`), extends along the path through `yoneda-from` (`J-from`), and `J-β` is a one-step value chain; the F0 suite's law 5 exhibits the same β on `refl` concretely.
There is no uniform primitive to get stuck: nothing in the system computes by induction on an open-ended type.

**The instructive inversion.** Each system's naive form fails at the _opposite end_, and each retrofit imports a piece of the other side:

|                       | computes on `refl`                   | computes on a non-trivial path                                                                                  | the retrofit                                                                                                                                |
| --------------------- | ------------------------------------ | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| CCHM `Path`           | no — regularity fails (§9.1, App. C) | yes — `comp`                                                                                                    | Swan's `Id` (§9.1): pair the path with a face formula `φ` marking _known constancy_; `r a = (1ₐ, 1_F)`; then `J d a (r a) = d` judgmentally |
| raw ua-base `PathInd` | yes — β on `[]`                      | no — F0 law 5 is PARTIAL exactly here: induction declines on every non-empty path over raw generating instances | saturation: instance stocks become modules over the path relation (design §3)                                                               |

Swan's construction is the cubical side conceding that a computing J needs the identity proof to carry _generated, syntactic data_ — and the mark lives exactly in the face lattice.
Saturation is the ua-base side conceding that eliminating along arbitrary paths needs _uniform transport structure_ — but imposed alphabet-first on the instance stock of each universal property, never as a kernel primitive over all types.
Read together: the ua-base path type is Swan's φ-marked fragment grown to be the entire type — all mark, no map.

## 5. Saturation, directly: the Bool worked example

The toy universe of `iu-26l` suffices: codes `{𝟙, ⊗, ⊕}`, and `Bool := 𝟙 ⊕ 𝟙`.

**The two paths.** At `(Bool, Bool)` the path protype contains the empty word `[]` (refl) and the one-letter word `swap` — the E-perm summand-swap generator applied at the top position.
Realization sends `here` to the identity and `swap` to negation, and the two are replay-distinct: `⟦swap⟧ = not ≉ʳ id`.
This is the U3.0c negation guard (`Negation.negation-distinct` in the demonstrator): the path protype must _not_ collapse to code equality, on pain of refuting completeness at `(Bool, Bool)` — the permanent witness that the system is univalent-not-truncated at the base stratum.

**The elimination problem.** Fix an instance stock `P` over paths out of `Bool` (the motive) and a diagonal value `d` at `(Bool, here)`.
J must produce a value at `(Bool, swap)`.

**The raw failure, empirically.** With the stock read as _raw generating instances_, β computes on `[]` — and at `swap` induction **declines**: no instance datum exists at a non-empty path, and nothing connects `d` to the `(Bool, swap)` fiber.
This is not hypothetical: it is the recorded PARTIAL of F0 law 5 (`law5_units.rs` — β holds on `refl`, and on a non-empty path the raw induction declines; behaviorally it declines on every non-empty path), the finding that forced the saturation invariant.

**The fix.** Saturate the alphabet: an instance is a _module element over the path relation_, whose free form is a generating instance together with an **absorbed path** (`SaturatedInstance { generator, absorbed, path_start }` in the F0 harness), with actions `actˡ`/`actʳ` and their laws (`act-idn*`, `act-seq*`, `act-xchg`).
The J value at `swap` is the diagonal value _with `swap` absorbed_:

```text
J(d) at swap  =  yoneda-from d .cmp swap  =  actʳ (d .cmp (endpoint)) swap
```

β at `[]` is one application of `act-idnʳ` — the absorbed path is empty, the action law collapses it, and the value is `d` definitionally (`J-β` is a one-step value chain). η is the action laws again (`J-η`, two steps; dinaturality).
The Rust probe `replay_path_ind_saturated` defines a value and agrees with β on empty paths — it confirms the absorbed path replays, then fires the base — the sufficiency half of the F0 finding (the Agda module action is the metatheoretic form; the probe is its store-level stand-in).

**The CCHM mirror, same example.** Transporting `d` from `refl` to a path `p` uses `comp` with the connection filler `⟨j⟩ p (i ∧ j)` — and that square _is_ the absorbed-path datum drawn as geometry: bottom edge degenerate at the start point, top and right edges `p`, the diagonal value being carried continuously along `p`.
At `p = swap` the composition computes.
At `p = refl` it runs along a degenerate system and sticks — regularity, again.
The four cells:

|                                  | at `refl`                       | at `swap`               |
| -------------------------------- | ------------------------------- | ----------------------- |
| CCHM: `comp` + connection square | stuck (propositional only)      | computes                |
| ua-base: saturated action        | `d` definitionally (`act-idnʳ`) | `actʳ d swap`, computes |

Where the interval spends geometry, the module spends algebra — and the failure corners swap places.

## 6. Connection = Yoneda

The two mechanisms of §5 are not analogous; they are the same theorem twice.
The unit UP _is_ the profunctor Yoneda correspondence (design §3): `yoneda-from w .cmp h = P .actʳ (w .cmp a) h` extends a diagonal value along the path by the module action, `yoneda-to` restricts to the identity, and the round trips are one action law and dinaturality.
CCHM's singleton contractibility is the same Yoneda extension rendered in the interval algebra, with the `∧`-connection supplying the extension square.
The economic difference is wholesale versus retail: the free De Morgan algebra makes _every_ degenerate square exist at once — an infinite coherence machine bought with the shape — while the polygraph pays coherence per stratum as finitely many completed 2-cells: R-cancel/R-comm/R-coxeter/R-coh, plus the three ratified errata that itemize the bill (`coh-dist-⊗unit`/`coh-dist-⊗assoc`, then `coh-dist-⊗comm`, then `coh-dist-⊕assoc` — the Laplaza cells landed erratum by erratum under explicit φ- and free-move analyses; design §4 and the execution record).
That itemized bill is the honest cost of the ua-base side, and the Squier completion tranche (`iu-c2h.1`) is where it is being paid.

**The placement, operationally — the face algebra runs inside the evaluator.** The wholesale-versus-retail contrast has a runtime face, and it is the sharpest form of the §3 placement thesis.
In Cubical Agda the decidable face algebra is consulted _inside the evaluator_: reducing `transp`/`hcomp` evaluates the cofibration and, on `Glue` and datatype lines, decomposes it into disjunctive normal form to fire the Kan step, while the type-checker's system-coverage check compares clause bodies on every pairwise face _overlap_ — the lattice meet. ua-base consults its decidable algebra only in the proof-equality _decision layer_: replay and realization are structural recursion on generators, and nothing in the transport loop calls an entailment procedure.
The primary-source verification of the Cubical Agda side, against the Agda `2.9.0`-nightly sources, is the register in §11 (claims V1–V4); the efficiency reading it licenses is §8.

## 7. Symmetry, directedness, and the temporal reading

CCHM's path reversal is the involution `(i/1−i)`: symmetry is baked into the shape algebra, every path is invertible structurally, and the theory cannot decline it — a directed cubical theory has to rebuild its interval.
`ℰ`'s generators are directed, and invertibility is per-generator _data_: the designated inverse schema with round-trip evidence (design §4).
The invertible lane is therefore certified rather than ambient, and the directed lane is native — directed J with symmetry underivable, exactly the LLV fragment's shape.

This is where the temporal reading attaches (the same axis `iu:docs/spec/ANALYSIS-abstract-stone-duality-contact.md` runs for ASD).
A CCHM path is a spatial gadget — a formal point of the continuum `[0,1]` mapped into a type; composition is geometric gluing; reversal is reflecting the interval, free of charge.
An edit string is a _process history_ — a run of the rewriting system; composition is concatenation of runs; and reversal of a process is not free: it exists exactly when every step ships an undo receipt, which is what the designated-inverse-plus-round-trip discipline says.
Replay-equivalence (ADR-69 D1) closes the loop: identity _of certificates_ is behavioral — two certificates are the same when they replay the same — so identification itself is a temporal notion, a recorded construction re-run, not a point of a path space.
Directedness is then not an exotic choice but the default of the temporal reading: histories have an arrow, and symmetry is the special (certified) case.
Space versus time is the placement thesis of §3 read philosophically: the interval presents identity as geometry; the polygraph presents it as history.

## 8. The price ledger

What CCHM buys: uniformity over an **open** universe — `Path A a b` at every type with no fence; univalence as a theorem via `Glue`, with the `∀i : F → F` quantifier elimination doing the real work in composition for glueing (§4.1, §6.2) — which universe composition reduces to (§7.1); higher inductive types.
What it pays: the interval, the face lattice, `comp`, and `Glue` enter the kernel as new trusted surface; J-on-refl is propositional; and the metatheory (normalization, canonicity) is external and heavyweight — the landscape chapter already prices this, with `xtt-2022`'s Corollary 8.4 bounding how much definitional equality a cubical kernel can carry before equality reflection, and the `ggms-2024`/`grubmuller-2026` pair as the empirical case study of the missing definitional J.

What ua-base buys: definitional J; no new trusted surface (no interval, no Kan primitive, no `Glue` — MLTT without K plus in-tree theorems); native directedness; per-stratum decidable `≈ᶜ` with ua computable in both directions.
What it pays: **fenced** completeness — μ-free, leaf-natural, and the fence is exact rather than cautious (the leaf-shift witness refutes completeness over unrestricted iso stocks at the first infinite leaf; design §2, §6) — plus the retail coherence bill of §6, plus stratified growth as a standing obligation rather than a one-time axiom.

Two calibration notes.
CCHM's own Kleene-algebra remark (§3, Remark: adding `i ∧ (1−i) ≤ j ∨ (1−j)` validates more judgmental equalities, still decidably) is the "which equalities are judgmental" dial turned inside the shape algebra; the ua-base grade discipline — β at `≈ʳ`, η at `≈ᶜ`, never `≡` — is the same dial made explicit in the statement (design §1).
And the decidable code universe is not the setoid/h-set retreat: codes are h-sets, but the protypes over them are not, the negation guard is the permanent in-tree witness (§5), and UIP-by-stealth is a named kill signal — the ua-base analogue of univalence keeping the universe from being a set.

**The efficiency reading (belief-grade; Agda-verified where cited).** Conditional on the Cubical Agda evaluator behaving as verified in §11, the efficiency edge of the ua-base placement is _architectural_, on three legs that degrade independently.
L1 — no constraint solving in the evaluator: ua-base replay/realization is structural recursion on generators plus concatenation, whereas every Cubical `transp`/`hcomp` step potentially runs the face decomposition and the `∀i` elimination (§11, V1–V4).
L2 — `refl` collapses definitionally: J-β is the empty-word constructor match (§3), so there is no analogue of degenerate transports left stuck as neutrals; the cubical side pays here in the documented, related forms of _empty systems_ (fought inside the evaluator by `ghcomp`) and composition-depth blow-up, with the geometrically-unavoidable failure of regularity underneath (§11, V5).
L3 — path-proof equality is excluded from conversion: β at replay grade, η at rule grade, never `≡` (design §1), so the word problem is pay-per-use, whereas cubical path equality _is_ definitional equality and every conversion check may pay for it.
L2 and L3 stand on the grade discipline and the constructor form of `refl` regardless of the evaluator details; L1 rests on the verified `transp`/`hcomp` reduction (§11, V2/V3).
The qualifiers stand unchanged by the verification: no benchmarks exist, the demonstrator is the leaf-free `{𝟙, ⊗, ⊕}` toy, and the claim is workload-relative — cubical's uniform overhead buys uniform generality over an open universe, and a workload that genuinely compares long path proofs stresses _our_ decision layer instead; decision-layer scaling at richer strata (μ-codes, binders, S2) is open.

The two systems' efficiency costs sit on opposite sides of the trust boundary — the price-ledger form of the placement thesis.
Cubical Agda's face machinery lives inside the trusted evaluator, so every optimization there is permanent trusted surface (its instance of the `xtt-2022`/Lean-GMP budgeted-fast-path lesson the landscape chapter already prices); ua-base's decision-layer costs live outside the TCB, or TCB-adjacent behind explicit witnesses, so the efficiency program can proceed without growing trust.
A concrete datum for the asymmetry: the cubical kernel _did_ carry Swan's `Id` as trusted surface — the `primIdJ` eliminator that makes J compute on `refl` (§4) — and has since shed it, removing `primIdJ` in Agda 2.6.3 (PR agda/agda#6032, "in favour of matching on the cubical identity type") and then the whole cubical `Id` type in Agda 2.8.0 (PR agda/agda#7652 with agda/cubical#1005, its "computational behaviour … exactly replicated by the user-definable identity type").
The retrofit §4 prices as the cubical concession to a computing J was, in the implementation, a trusted primitive the kernel ultimately preferred to retire (§11).

**The retirement chronology, read as evidence (belief-grade timeline reading; the two removals themselves are the §11-verified facts).** The chronology matters because it rules out the "belated realization" reading: Swan's construction was available from the beginning — it _is_ CCHM §9.1 — so the initial Cubical Agda shipped its direct transcription, a dedicated trusted primitive carrying the (path, constancy-cofibration) pairing with `primIdJ` as the baked-in eliminator.
What did not yet exist in mature form was the general machinery that would make that primitive redundant: derived Kan operations for arbitrary indexed inductive families, and without-K dependent pattern matching elaborated through the cubical structure.
The retirement then tracked that machinery's maturation in stages — 2.6.3 could retire only the eliminator (replacing `primIdJ` with matching on the still-builtin `Id`), and only 2.8.0 could claim the fully user-definable inductive family replicates the builtin's computational behaviour exactly — a multi-release arc whose length is the measure of how hard-won "the generic treatment already yields one" was.
The kernel-evolution pattern is the transferable lesson for our own ledger: the special case you can justify today ships as trusted surface and sheds only when general machinery provably subsumes it, so trusted-surface minimization is a real, observed pressure inside cubical kernels — but one that pays out on a release-cycle horizon, not on demand.
Two boundaries the subsumption did not move, priced here so the datum is not over-read.
Semantically, the inductive `Id` and `Path` remain distinct: propositionally equivalent, definitionally incomparable — strict J on one side, definitional endpoint and funext behaviour on the other — and the gap is fixed by the regularity no-go (§11, V5), not by engineering effort still to be spent.
Practically, `Path` is therefore not a drop-in replacement for `Id` but a different primitive that redistributes which equations are judgmental, and every Book-HoTT development written against `Id`'s grain feels that seam when ported.
That seam — together with the documented performance wall (§11, V5) and, recalled here but _not_ verified this pass, the model-theoretic reservation that the De Morgan cubical model was shown not to present classical homotopy types (with the equivariant cartesian line as the repair, in a different cubical theory than Agda implements) — is an honest reading of why cubical adoption in the HoTT community stayed partial: interpretive, priced as such.

## 9. The unintended vindication, and a Brouwerian gloss

**(a) Levitation and polygraphs, vindicated together.** Neither ingredient was adopted for the identity story: levitation entered for datatype declarations (description codes; the S2 bill on the wyrd side), polygraphs for rewriting and fusion (the doctrine fragment; the L2 engine on the gandr side).
The comparison shows they are exactly the two halves a computing, univalent identity needs under the placement thesis: a **recursable universe** — one cannot compute identity by recursion over an open universe, but a levitated universe is an inductive object one can recurse over — and a **generated identity** — so that elimination is structural, `refl` is a detectable constructor, and the eliminator never consults a stuck uniform primitive.
That the two prior commitments compose into precisely the CCHM-complement is the vindication: the design was over-determined from independent engineering decisions.

**(b) The OTT objection, dissolved rather than rebutted.** The classical objection to Observational Type Theory's closed universe is real but aims at _meta-closure_: OTT fixes the grammar of type formers once, so extending the language means redefining the equality function — closure of the **theory**.
Levitation closes the **signature** while leaving the data open: a new datatype is a new value of the description code type, not a new type former, and the identity-by-recursion function is written once, over the codes.
Closed at the object level, open at the data level — the objection's target is gone, not argued away. (OTT's other horn, proof irrelevance/UIP, is declined separately: §8's untruncated protypes.)

**(c) A Brouwerian gloss — interpretive, priced as such.** The resulting universe is a _potential_ object in something close to Brouwer's sense: at every stratum a completed, surveyable, decidable presentation; growth by lawlike stages (stratified fullness — `ua_n` per stratum, the fence of §8 moving outward; on the kernel side, subset growth as a standing per-phase obligation) rather than an ambient actual totality given all at once.
And identification is temporal construction — certificates are recorded constructions, replay is re-construction, identity of certificates is sameness of replays (§7) — where the cubical picture is spatial: identifications as paths in a continuum, coherence as geometry.
The gloss claims Brouwerian _temperament_ — potential versus actual, time as the substrate of construction — and nothing further: no choice sequences, no continuity principles, and no creating-subject arguments are imported or licensed by it.
Read this way, the closed universe is not a concession extracted by decidability but the positive content of the position: the universe one _constructs in stages_ was never going to be an actual infinite object, and the CCHM comparison locates precisely what that stance costs (§8) and buys (§4).
