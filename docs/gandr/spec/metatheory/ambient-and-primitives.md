## The cubical contact

Both systems put a finitely presented decidable algebra at the bottom of the identity story; the placement differs ([[../metatheory#The placement thesis|the placement thesis]]).
The operational contrasts, each verified where marked:

**The J ledger.** Cubical loses J-on-refl because J is derived through singleton contractibility and its computation rule on refl reduces to a composition along a degenerate system; making that vanish is regularity, which fails _geometrically_ — a feature of the model, not an engineering gap [@cohen-coquand-huber-mortberg-2018-cubical]. gandr keeps it because elimination is structural recursion on the word and there is no uniform primitive to get stuck.
Each naive form fails at the opposite end and each retrofit imports a piece of the other side: the cubical retrofit (a path paired with a constancy cofibration, with a computing eliminator) concedes that a computing J needs the identity proof to carry generated syntactic data; the saturation retrofit (instance stocks as modules over the path relation) concedes that eliminating along arbitrary paths needs uniform transport structure — imposed alphabet-first on instance stocks, never as a kernel primitive over all types. gandr's path type is the constancy-marked fragment grown to be the entire type: all mark, no map.

**Connection = Yoneda.** The two transport mechanisms are the same theorem twice: the unit universal property is the profunctor Yoneda correspondence, and the cubical connection square is the same extension rendered in interval algebra.
The economics are wholesale versus retail: the free De Morgan algebra makes every degenerate square exist at once — an infinite coherence machine bought with the shape — while the polygraph pays coherence per stratum as finitely many completed cells.
The dependent Yoneda lemma of the synthetic line is the same identification one rung up, with its coherence likewise bought with an interval.

**The verified evaluator register** (adversarially checked against a pinned Agda checkout and its tracker; retained at claim grade):

* the face algebra runs _inside the evaluator_: partial/system clauses are checked pairwise on face overlaps at type-checking time; transport carries a constancy cofibration consulted during reduction; homogeneous composition reduces on the full face; the Glue transport runs the face-quantifier elimination;
* regularity is absent and geometrically unavoidable; its documented costs are empty-system litter (fought inside the evaluator by a dedicated variant) and composition-depth blow-up on high-dimensional cubes;
* the kernel _did_ carry the computing-J retrofit as a trusted primitive and retired it in stages across two major releases, only once general machinery provably subsumed it — the empirical datum that trusted-surface minimization is a real, observed pressure inside cubical kernels, paying out on a release-cycle horizon, not on demand.

**The price ledger.** Cubical buys uniformity over an open universe, univalence as a theorem, and higher inductive types; it pays the interval, the face lattice, composition, and Glue as permanent trusted surface, propositional J-on-refl, and heavyweight external metatheory. gandr buys definitional J, no new trusted surface, native directedness, and per-stratum decidability; it pays fenced completeness (the leaf-natural, recursion-free fence, exact rather than cautious), the retail coherence bill, and stratified growth as a standing obligation.
The efficiency reading (architectural, no benchmarks, workload-relative): no constraint solving in the evaluator; refl collapses definitionally; path-proof equality excluded from conversion, so the word problem is pay-per-use — and the two systems' efficiency costs sit on opposite sides of the trust boundary, so gandr's efficiency program can proceed without growing trust.

Span-based internal parametricity avoids fibrancy outright [@altenkirch-chamoun-kaposi-shulman-2024-internal]; geometry there is emergent, not primitive — the one line that directly addresses the no-interval constraint.
The organizing reframe: choose a base category and a fibrancy notion (Kan for homotopy; discrete for parametricity; Segal/covariant/Rezk and plausibly Conduché for directedness; clock-irrelevant for guarded; the Schanuel topos for nominal), and the operator zoo is downstream, organized by the transpension right adjoint with its affine-variable costs [@nuyts-devriese-2024-transpension]; a shape-theory-parameterized system is the same idea at the syntax level.

**Where a modality is genuinely needed**: expressing _globality_ of a universe — the internal language cannot say "global element", and reflexive-graph models interpret every former canonically _except_ the universe [@licata-orton-pitts-spitters-2018-internal-universes]. gandr is exempt because the code universe is Tarski-style data — and not accidentally: a levitated universe is an inductive object one can recurse over, which is the load-bearing precondition of computing identity by recursion at all.
The exemption ends the moment the universe stops being a code family (a Russell universe, "the type of all Segal presheaves", closure under a former quantifying over the universe itself).

## The technology cluster

The parametricity line is adopted as a technology source.
The alignment (each row a stated motivation of that line and a standing commitment of this one): types stratified by finite dimensions; strict equality unfeared, coherence obligations feared; models that need not present spaces but must compute; naturality for free (against the hand-carried naturality field and its per-degree cost); variance sorted out (against the priced variance axis).
The recorded fork: factorization systems are gandr's idiom and explicitly not that line's.
The implementation landscape: univalence and internal parametricity coexist in a shipping typechecker (the bridges extension typechecks the cubical standard library and validates relativity) at cubical's trusted-surface price — "priced and shipping", against the observational line where the two modes are mutually exclusive by flag and their combination is _unspecified theory_; the modality-free implementation implements the span-based route in full and the displayed route only on closed terms, which is why the internal-diagram tooling is one modality short of its paper form.
Transport-does-not-compute-for-datatypes in the leading observational implementation is evidence _for_ the placement thesis: the thing it cannot yet do for datatypes is the thing gandr moved out of the type formers and into a carried certificate.
A computational univalence certificate's field list there (four transport/lifting fields plus a corecursive higher field) is the concrete specification the invertible core's certificates must be compared against — re-scoped to the core-coincidence obligation, since the forward-only directed shape is a decision.

**The nominal aim.** Nullary internal parametricity _is_ name abstraction — an identification, not an analogy — with a nominal type theory built on it (α-equivalence as equality, a closed induction principle, good behaviour under weakening), the Schanuel topos as semantic confirmation, and higher-order abstract syntax proved adequate from parametricity alone where previous proofs needed strictly stronger Kripke parametricity [@vanmuylder-nuyts-devriese-2025-nominal] [@vanmuylder-2026-thesis].
Adopted as an aim because gandr's applications are metatheory and reasoning about systems, where object languages with binders are the normal case, and circuit-shaped cells make the hostable object languages substantially more expressive; the alternative (sharing becomes a wire) keeps the routes genuine alternatives.

**Parametricity as coherence management** has five independent arrivals — cohesion (which strictly contains the synthetic-Tait setup, the residue being connectedness, which is what buys induction-principle derivation) [@aberle-2024-cohesion], displayed type theory, cubical internal parametricity [@cavallo-harper-2021-internal-parametricity], the pretype-theory line, and the ω-categorical naturality meta-operation [@benjamin-markakis-offord-sarti-vicary-2025-naturality] — a pattern to act on.
Under the trusted-surface criterion the cubical-parametricity system is admissible and priced; the cohesion system is deferred pending canonicity (its computation rules are rewrite pragmas with canonicity conjectured); the synthetic system stays blocked (axiomatized interval, no computational interpretation).

## The temporal reading

Identity is a construction in time over an unfinished substrate: a presheaf _is_ observation-on-demand (only values at probed shapes are ever held); the certificate built by degree is the agreement so far; Reedy degree is inductive fuel against the coinductive presheaf — one structure, not two; staged certification is the per-degree Segal check with equivariant latching and matching; the cost of unfolding is a computed count of naturality checks; and certificate composition follows the two-mode discipline.
The programme's independently attested shape: the analytic-functor slogans hold at each finite groupoid level only by passing to the next — the usual infinite ladder, self-contained only at ∞ — so the design must either stay Σ-free (in the representation, where gandr does) or carry symmetry as witness data at a fixed level; it may not expect the tower to close.
Two applied readings are kept: containers (the finite-position layout case computes before any extensionality machinery exists; extensionality proper is tabulation of a pointwise certificate, paid per observation), and protocols (an equivalence between session types is a carried certificate whose transport is an adapter process; proof relevance operational, the directed family matching deprecation reality, the cost hook a compatibility budget) — with the standing seam warning that a data stratum and a session stratum interoperating crosses an ordering boundary that is known not to be conservative, to be flagged before either stratum hardens.
